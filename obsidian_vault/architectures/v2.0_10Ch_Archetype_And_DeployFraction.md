# 🚀 Architecture v2.0 — 10-Channel Unit-Archetype CNN & 4-Head Batch-Fraction Actor-Critic

Back to [[00_Index_MOC|Vault Index (MOC)]] • Supersedes [[v1.0_Baseline_8Ch_SingleDeploy]] • Encoding Spec: [[Unit_Type_Spatial_Encoding]]

---

## 1. Summary of Architectural Upgrades

Architecture **v2.0** introduces two high-impact structural improvements to `CoCSpatialEncoder` and `CoCActorCritic`:

1. **4th Factorized Action Head — Troop Deployment Fraction ($\alpha_{\text{frac}}$)**:
   - Adds `deploy_fraction_head: Linear(320 -> 5)` over discrete fraction bins $\mathcal{F} = \{0.0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00\}$.
   - When `a_type == 1` (`DeployTroop`), the environment spawns:
     $$N_{\text{spawn}} = \max\!\Big(1,\; \min\!\big(N_{\text{avail}},\; \text{round}(\alpha_{\text{frac}} \cdot N_{\text{avail}})\big)\Big)$$
   - Cuts the time required to drop a 14-Thrower main core from $8.96\text{s}$ ($14$ steps) down to **$0.64\text{s}$ ($1$ step with $\alpha_{\text{frac}}=1.0$)** or a 2-flank split ($2$ steps with $\alpha_{\text{frac}}=0.5$).
2. **Expanded `10 × 50 × 50` Spatial Observation Tensor**:
   - **Channel 8 (`Allied Unit Type Signature`)**: Encodes the tactical archetype $\phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ of friendly troops, siege machines, pets, and heroes at each $(x, y)$ tile via dominant-unit max-pooling.
   - **Channel 9 (`Enemy Unit Type Signature`)**: Encodes the tactical archetype $\phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ of defending Clan Castle troops, defending Heroes, TH18 Guardians, and triggered Skeleton Traps — with **Ground Skeletons (`0.10`)** and **Air Skeletons (`0.28`)** encoded as strictly distinct signatures.

## 2. Full 10-Channel Spatial Tensor Specification (`10 × 50 × 50`)

| Channel | Name | Mathematical Normalization | Tactical Role |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `0` | **Building HP Ratio** | $\text{HP}_{x,y} / \text{HP}_{\max} \in [0, 1]$ | Surviving structure footprints & remaining durability. |
| `1` | **Defense DPS Threat** | $\min(\text{DPS}_{x,y} / 1000, 1.0)$ | Lethal kill-zones (Monolith, Eagle, Inferno, Scattershot, Hot Candle). |
| `2` | **Town Hall 18 Mask** | $\mathbb{I}[(x,y) \in \text{TH18}] \in \{0, 1\}$ | Marks the $4\times 4$ Town Hall 18 core required for the 2nd Victory Star. |
| `3` | **Wall Integrity** | $\text{HP}_{\text{wall}} / \text{HP}_{\text{wall,max}} \in [0, 1]$ | Closed compartments vs. breached wall segments. |
| `4` | **No-Deploy Red Zone** | $1.0 = \text{forbidden},\; 0.0 = \text{legal}$ | Dynamic 1-tile building halo restricting troop/hero/siege spawns. |
| `5` | **Friendly Ground HP** | $\min(\sum_{u \in G} \text{HP}_u / 5000, 1.0)$ | Total HP mass of friendly ground units at tile $(x,y)$. |
| `6` | **Friendly Air HP** | $\min(\sum_{u \in A} \text{HP}_u / 5000, 1.0)$ | Total HP mass of friendly air units at tile $(x,y)$. |
| `7` | **Active Spell Field** | $\min(\text{ticks}_{\text{rem}} / 300, 1.0)$ | Active Rage, Heal, Freeze, Invisibility, Poison, Overgrowth zones. |
| `8` | **Allied Unit Type Signature** | $\max_{u \in \text{Ally}(x,y)} \phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ | Identifies **what kind** of friendly unit occupies $(x,y)$ (Swarm vs. Ranged Core vs. Tank vs. Healer vs. Hero). |
| `9` | **Enemy Unit Type Signature** | $\max_{u \in \text{Enemy}(x,y)} \phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ | Identifies defending CC troops, defending Heroes, TH18 Guardians, and **Ground Skeletons (`0.10`) vs. Air Skeletons (`0.28`)**. |

## 3. Layer-by-Layer Parameter Count (`v2.0`)

- **Stem Conv1 (`s1`)**: `Conv2d(10, 32, kernel_size=3, padding=1)` + `GroupNorm(4, 32)` + `SiLU`
  - Params: $(10 \times 32 \times 9) + 32 + 64 = 2{,}976$ (up from $2{,}368$ in `v1.0`, $+608$ params).
- **Spatial Tower (`s2` + `s3` Dilated ResBlock)**: $18{,}560 + 73{,}984 = 92{,}544$ params.
- **Global Tower (`g1`..`g5`) + Context MLP (`c1`..`c2`)**: $742{,}592$ params.
- **Actor Heads**:
  - `slot_type_head`: `Linear(320, 7)` $\to 2{,}247$ params
  - `slot_idx_head`: `Linear(320, 10)` $\to 3{,}210$ params
  - `deploy_fraction_head`: `Linear(320, 5)` $\to 1{,}605$ params
  - `tile_conv` + `tile_ctx_proj`: $65 + 20{,}544 = 20{,}609$ params
- **Critic Head**: $32{,}449$ params
- **Total Trainable Parameters**: **`878,236`** (only $+0.18\%$ parameter overhead over `v1.0`!).

## 4. Backward-Compatible Weight Migration (`8ch -> 10ch`)

To preserve training investment across checkpoints saved under `v1.0`:
- When loading an `8-channel` checkpoint into `v2.0` (`in_channels=10`), `_load_state_dict_flexible` copies the learned $(32, 8, 3, 3)$ stem filters into channels `0..7` of `spatial_stem.0.weight` and initializes channels `8..9` with low-variance Kaiming weights.
