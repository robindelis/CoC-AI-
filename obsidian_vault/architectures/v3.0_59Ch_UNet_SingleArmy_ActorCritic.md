## 1. Architectural Overview (`v3.0` U-Net Edition)

Architecture **v3.0 (U-Net)** (`2,480,440` trainable parameters) features an end-to-end multi-scale encoder-decoder backbone paired with autoregressive categorical action heads and a high-resolution spatial deployment pointer:

1. **Multi-Scale Contracting-Expansive Spatial Backbone (ADR-011)**:
   - Replaces the 3-block Dilated ResNet with a 3-level contracting-expansive U-Net topology.
   - **Contracting Pathway (Downsampling)**: $50 \times 50 \to 25 \times 25 \to 12 \times 12 \to 6 \times 6$ bottleneck. Expands the receptive field to cover the entire $50 \times 50$ battlefield at the bottleneck while eliminating dilation gridding artifacts.
   - **Expansive Pathway (Upsampling with Skips)**: $6 \times 6 \to 12 \times 12 \to 25 \times 25 \to 50 \times 50$. High-resolution skip connections directly link early encoder layers to decoder upsampling blocks, preserving exact single-tile wall breaches, trap proximity, and building perimeters.

2. **Branched Global Tower Routing**:
   - The Global Tower branches off from the partially upscaled $12 \times 12$ expansive feature map (`d3`, 128 channels) rather than the final spatial map.
   - Captures high-level abstracted base-wide tactical features for strategic categorical decisions (`slot_type`, `slot_idx`, `deploy_fraction`, and value critic) without wasting parameters on dense projections from $50 \times 50$.

3. **High-Resolution Spatial Tile Pointer Head**:
   - The full-resolution $50 \times 50 \times 32$ spatial feature map `spatial_features` is modulated by the selected action type and slot index via FiLM conditioning (`tile_cond_proj: Linear(273, 32)`).
   - A 2-layer convolutional head (`Conv2d(32, 32, 3, p=1) -> SiLU -> Conv2d(32, 1, 1)`) computes fine-grained logits across all 2,500 grid tiles.

4. **Single-Army Specialist Training & Checkpoint Export (ADR-005)**:
   - Each checkpoint is dedicated to a specific army composition across the 5 TH18 reference villages (`Central Box`, `Offset Diamond`, `Anti-Hydra Ring`, `Anti-Fireball`, `Teaser Killzone`).

5. **59-Channel Fine-Grained Factorization (ADR-008)**:
   - Complete non-overlapping representation for base geometry, 12 loadout units, 4 friendly heroes, 4 friendly spells, 9 defending units (including all 6 individual defending heroes), 22 defense types + Clan Castle, and 3 non-offensive building groups.

6. **Live Available Inventory Vector (`army_context`, `(B, 64)`) (ADR-003)**:
   - Projected via `Linear(64 -> 256) + LayerNorm + SiLU` and fused with the spatial global embedding.
