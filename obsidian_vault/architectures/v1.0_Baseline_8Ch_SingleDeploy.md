# 🏗️ Architecture v1.0 — Baseline 8-Channel CNN & Single-Unit Deployment

Back to [[00_Index_MOC|Vault Index (MOC)]] • Superseded by [[v2.0_10Ch_Archetype_And_DeployFraction]]

---

## 1. Input Observation Space

- **Spatial Grid Tensor**: $S_t \in [0, 1]^{8 \times 50 \times 50}$
  - `Ch 0`: Building HP ratio ($\text{HP} / \text{HP}_{\max}$)
  - `Ch 1`: Defense DPS threat ($\min(\text{DPS} / 1000, 1)$)
  - `Ch 2`: Town Hall 18 binary mask ($\{0, 1\}$)
  - `Ch 3`: Wall HP ratio ($\text{HP}_{\text{wall}} / \text{HP}_{\text{wall,max}}$)
  - `Ch 4`: No-Deploy Red Zone mask ($1.0 = \text{forbidden}, 0.0 = \text{legal}$)
  - `Ch 5`: Friendly Ground HP density ($\min(\sum \text{HP}_u / 5000, 1.0)$)
  - `Ch 6`: Friendly Air HP density ($\min(\sum \text{HP}_u / 5000, 1.0)$)
  - `Ch 7`: Active Spell field
- **Global Army Context Vector**: $c_t \in [0, 1]^{64}$

---

## 2. Output Action Space (3 Factorized Heads)

$$
\pi_\theta(a_t \mid s_t) = \pi_\theta(a_{\text{type}} \mid s_t) \cdot \pi_\theta(a_{\text{idx}} \mid s_t)^{u_{\text{idx}}} \cdot \pi_\theta(a_{x,y} \mid s_t)^{u_{\text{tile}}}
$$

- `slot_type_head`: $\mathbb{R}^{320} \to \mathbb{R}^7$ (`NoOp`, `DeployTroop`, `DeployHero`, `HeroAbility`, `DeploySiege`, `PopSiege`, `CastSpell`)
- `slot_idx_head`: $\mathbb{R}^{320} \to \mathbb{R}^{10}$
- `tile_conv`: $\mathbb{R}^{64 \times 50 \times 50} \to \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 50 \times 50} \to \mathbb{R}^{2500}$

---

## 3. Observed Limitations in v1.0 (Why We Upgraded to v2.0)

1. **Multi-Unit Deployment Bottleneck ($0.64\text{s}$ per unit)**:
   - Because each RL step advances the simulation by `ticks_per_step = 8` ($0.64\text{s}$) and `DeployTroop` spawned only **1 troop per action**, deploying a stack of $14$ Throwers or $24$ Balloons took $14 \times 0.64\text{s} = 8.96\text{s}$ to $15.36\text{s}$ of combat time.
   - Early troops walked into Eagle Artillery / Monolith range alone and died before the rest of the squad even spawned.
2. **Unit-Identity Blindness in Channels 5 & 6**:
   - Channels 5 and 6 only summed HP density. The CNN could not tell whether a ground cluster on tile $(x, y)$ was a pack of **Root Riders** (wall-crushing tanks), **Throwers** (long-range ranged core that needs Rage + Healers), or **Wall Breakers**.
3. **Invisibility of Defending Troops, Heroes & Skeleton Traps**:
   - Defending Clan Castle troops (e.g., Super Minions, Ice Golems), defending Heroes (Archer Queen, Minion Prince, Dragon Duke), and triggered **Skeleton Traps** (both **Ground Skeletons** and **Air Skeletons**) had **zero representation** in the spatial tensor!
   - Consequently, the agent could not aim `Poison` spells at defending Clan Castle troops or distinguish harmless Ground Skeletons under an air Dragon push from Air Skeletons swarming Balloons.
