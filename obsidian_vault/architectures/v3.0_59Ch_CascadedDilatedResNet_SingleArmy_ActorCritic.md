## 1. Architectural Overview (`v3.0` Enhanced)

Architecture **v3.0** (`1,481,080` trainable parameters) is designed around four core principles:

1. **Single-Army Specialist Training & Checkpoint Export (ADR-005)**:
   - Each model is trained exclusively for **one dedicated Army Composition** across the 5 TH18 reference villages (`Central Box`, `Offset Diamond`, `Anti-Hydra Ring`, `Anti-Fireball`, `Teaser Killzone`).
   - Eliminates cross-army gradient interference between mechanically opposite strategies (e.g., ground Thrower Smash vs. air Dragon Duke Hydra).

2. **59-Channel Fine-Grained Factorization (ADR-008)**:
   - **Group 1: Base & Environment (`Ch 0..4`)**: Building HP ratio, Defense DPS threat, Town Hall 18 footprint, Wall integrity, No-Deploy Red Zone.
   - **Group 2: Friendly Troops, Siege & CC (`Ch 5..16`)**: Dedicated channel per loadout unit (Thrower, Healer, Furnace, Druid/Bear, Ice Golem, Super Wall Breaker, Headhunter, Balloon, Archer, Log Launcher, CC Super Yeti, CC Super Valkyrie), normalized by `count / max_in_army` on tile.
   - **Group 3: Friendly Heroes (`Ch 17..20`)**: Dedicated channel per hero (Barbarian King, Archer Queen, Grand Warden, Royal Champion), strictly `1.0` if alive on tile.
   - **Group 4: Friendly Spells (`Ch 21..24`)**: Totem Spell, plus Rage, Poison, and Skeleton Spell continuous `1.0 -> 0.0` fading values covering the full circular Area of Effect.
   - **Group 5: Enemy Defending Units (`Ch 25..33`)**:
     - `Ch 25`: Defending CC & Guardian
     - `Ch 26..31`: **Individual Defending Heroes**: Barbarian King (`Ch 26`), Archer Queen (`Ch 27`), Grand Warden (`Ch 28`), Royal Champion (`Ch 29`), Minion Prince (`Ch 30`), Dragon Duke (`Ch 31`).
     - `Ch 32..33`: Enemy Ground & Air Skeletons.
   - **Group 6: 22 Dedicated Defense Types & Clan Castle (`Ch 34..55`)**: One channel per TH18 defense type plus `Ch 55: Clan Castle`.
   - **Group 7: 3 Non-Offensive Building Groups (`Ch 56..58`)**:
     - `Ch 56`: Storages & Reservoirs + Hero Hall (Gold, Elixir, Dark Elixir Storages + Hero Hall HP ratio in [0, 1]).
     - `Ch 57`: Resource Collectors (Gold Mines, Elixir Collectors, Dark Elixir Drills HP ratio in [0, 1]).
     - `Ch 58`: Utility & Other Buildings (Army Camps, Barracks, Laboratory, Spell Factories, Blacksmith, etc. HP ratio in [0, 1]).

3. **Cascaded Dilated ResNet Spatial Backbone & Lean Global Tower**:
   - Replaced bloated `AdaptiveAvgPool2d((4, 4)) + Linear(2048, 256)` (which consumed 54.3% of the entire network in a single dense matrix) with Global Average Pooling `AdaptiveAvgPool2d((1, 1)) + Linear(128, 256)` (saving 491,520 dense weights).
   - Reinvested parameters into a 3-block cascaded Dilated ResNet (`DilatedResBlock` with dilations $d=1, 2, 4$), tripling the spatial receptive field from $15\times 15$ to $39\times 39$ across the 50×50 board.
   - Conditioned spatial tile pointer `tile_cond_proj` on `[global_emb || type_onehot || idx_onehot]` (273-D) for slot-specific FiLM modulation.

4. **Live Available Inventory Vector (`army_context`, `(B, 64)`) (ADR-003)**:
   - Tracks remaining deployables, hero abilities, and siege states, zeroing out depleted slots.
