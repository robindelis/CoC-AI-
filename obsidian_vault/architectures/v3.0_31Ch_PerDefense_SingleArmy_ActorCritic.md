## 1. Architectural Overview (`v3.0`)

Architecture **v3.0** (`1,375,288` trainable parameters) is designed around three core principles:

1. **Single-Army Specialist Training & Checkpoint Export (ADR-005)**:
   - Each model is trained exclusively for **one dedicated Army Composition** across the 5 TH18 reference villages (`Central Box`, `Offset Diamond`, `Anti-Hydra Ring`, `Anti-Fireball`, `Teaser Killzone`).
   - Eliminates cross-army gradient interference between mechanically opposite strategies (e.g., ground Thrower Smash vs. air Dragon Duke Hydra).
   - **Default Checkpoint Naming**: `{army_slug}_ep{episodes}_{best_pct}pct_{best_stars}star.pt` (e.g., `thrower_healer_totem_smash_ep1918_100pct_3star.pt`).
   - **Exported Army Metadata**: Every `.pt` checkpoint embeds the full `army` loadout dictionary (`name`, `troops`, `spells`, `heroes`, `hero_equipment`, `siege_machine`, `cc_troops`, `cc_spells`) so the **AI Model Tester** automatically equips the exact composition the policy was trained on.

2. **50-Channel Dedicated Spatial Observation Tensor `(B, 50, 50, 50)` (ADR-007)**:
   - Replaces earlier 10-channel and 31-channel coarse aggregations (which merged troops into generic ground/air densities and archetype signatures) with **50 uncompressed, dedicated channels**:
     - **Group 1: Base & Environment (`Ch 0..4`)**: Building HP ratio, Defense DPS threat, Town Hall 18 footprint, Wall integrity, No-Deploy Red Zone.
     - **Group 2: Friendly Troops, Siege & CC (`Ch 5..16`)**: Dedicated channel per loadout unit (Thrower, Healer, Furnace, Druid/Bear, Ice Golem, Super Wall Breaker, Headhunter, Balloon, Archer, Log Launcher, CC Super Yeti, CC Super Valkyrie), normalized by `count / max_in_army` on tile.
     - **Group 3: Friendly Heroes (`Ch 17..20`)**: Dedicated channel per hero (Barbarian King, Archer Queen, Grand Warden, Royal Champion), strictly `1.0` if alive on tile.
     - **Group 4: Friendly Spells (`Ch 21..24`)**: Totem Spell (summoned 1×1 unit HP ratio `u.hp / u.max_hp`), plus Rage, Poison, and Skeleton Spell continuous `1.0 -> 0.0` fading values covering the full circular Area of Effect.
     - **Group 5: Enemy Defending Units (`Ch 25..28`)**: Defending CC & TH18 Guardians (`min(N / 5.0, 1.0)`), Defending Heroes (`1.0`), Defending Ground Skeletons (`min(N / 10.0, 1.0)`), and Defending Air Skeletons (`min(N / 10.0, 1.0)`).
     - **Group 6: 21 Dedicated Defense Types (`Ch 29..49`)**: One channel per TH18 defense type (`ArcherTower` through `CraftingStation / Hot Candle`), preserving all switchable mode encodings, directional cone angles, and Hidden Tesla proximity masking.
   - Feeding 50 channels into `Conv2d(50, 64, kernel_size=3, dilation=2)` corresponds to a learned 64-D embedding lookup per feature plane convolved over the $50\times 50$ map, adding only $10{,}944$ parameters ($0.80\%$ increase over 31-channel predecessor) while completely resolving multi-unit tile overlap ambiguity.

3. **Live Available Inventory Vector (`army_context`, `(B, 64)`) (ADR-003)**:
   - Even with a fixed training army recipe, the policy must know at every decision step $t$ **which troops, spells, heroes, equipment abilities, and siege machine are still available to deploy and in what remaining quantity**.
   - `extract_army_context()` outputs a `64-D` vector projected via `army_context_proj: Linear(64 -> 256) + LayerNorm(256) + SiLU` and fused residually (`global_emb = global_emb + army_context_proj(army_context)`). Once a troop or spell slot's remaining count reaches `0`, both its ID and count entries become `0.0`.
