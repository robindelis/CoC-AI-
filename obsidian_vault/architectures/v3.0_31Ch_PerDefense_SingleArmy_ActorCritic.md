## 1. Architectural Overview (`v3.0`)

Architecture **v3.0** (`1,364,344` trainable parameters) is designed around three core principles:

1. **Single-Army Specialist Training & Checkpoint Export**:
   - Each model is trained exclusively for **one dedicated Army Composition** across the 5 TH18 reference villages (`Central Box`, `Offset Diamond`, `Anti-Hydra Ring`, `Anti-Fireball`, `Teaser Killzone`).
   - Eliminates cross-army gradient interference between mechanically opposite strategies (e.g., ground Thrower Smash vs. air Dragon Duke Hydra).
   - **Default Checkpoint Naming**: `{army_slug}_ep{episodes}_{best_pct}pct_{best_stars}star.pt` (e.g., `thrower_healer_totem_smash_ep60_94pct_3star.pt`).
   - **Exported Army Metadata**: Every `.pt` checkpoint embeds the full `army` loadout dictionary (`name`, `troops`, `spells`, `heroes`, `hero_equipment`, `siege_machine`, `cc_troops`, `cc_spells`) so the **AI Model Tester** automatically equips the exact composition the policy was trained on.

2. **31-Channel Per-Defense Spatial Observation Tensor `(B, 31, 50, 50)`**:
   - **Channels `0..9`**: Base structure durability, DPS threat, Town Hall 18 footprint, Wall integrity, No-Deploy Red Zone, friendly Ground/Air HP density, active Spell zones, and Allied/Enemy Unit Archetype Signatures $\phi(u) \in [0, 1]$.
   - **Channels `10..30` (21 Dedicated Defense Type Channels)**: One `50 × 50` channel per TH18 defensive building type (`ArcherTower` through `CraftingStation / Hot Candle`).
   - Feeding 21 per-defense channels into `Conv2d(31, 64, kernel_size=3, dilation=2)` is mathematically equivalent to a learned 64-D spatial embedding lookup per defense type followed by a $3\times 3$ convolution, while adding only $21 \times 64 \times 9 = 12{,}096$ parameters ($\approx 0.89\%$ of the model).
   - **Mode & Directional Encoding**: Switchable defenses encode their active mode (`InfernoTower`: `1.0` Single vs `0.5` Multi; `XBow`: `1.0` Air+Ground vs `0.5` Ground; `MultiGearTower`: `1.0` Long Range vs `0.5` Fast Attack; `SpellTower`: `0.25` Rage, `0.50` Poison, `0.75` Invisibility, `1.00` Earthquake), directional defenses (`AirSweeper`, `Firespitter`) encode their facing angle in `[0.25, 1.0]`, and `HiddenTesla` is masked to `0.0` across all channels until revealed.

3. **Live Available Inventory Vector (`army_context`, `(B, 64)`)**:
   - Even with a fixed training army recipe, the policy must know at every decision step $t$ **which troops, spells, heroes, equipment abilities, and siege machine are still available to deploy and in what remaining quantity**.
   - `extract_army_context()` outputs a `64-D` vector projected via `army_context_proj: Linear(64 -> 256) + LayerNorm(256) + SiLU` and fused residually (`global_emb = global_emb + army_context_proj(army_context)`). Once a troop or spell slot's remaining count reaches `0`, both its ID and count entries become `0.0`.

## 2. Complete 31-Channel Spatial Tensor Specification (`(B, 31, 50, 50)`)

| Ch # | Channel Name | Value / Normalization on Footprint Tiles | Tactical Role |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `0` | **Building HP Ratio** | $\text{HP}_{x,y} / 11000.0 \in [0, 1]$ | Surviving structure footprints & remaining durability (Hidden Tesla masked until revealed). |
| `1` | **Defense DPS Threat** | $\min(\text{DPS}_{x,y} / 750.0, 1.0)$ | Lethal kill-zones across all active defenses. |
| `2` | **Town Hall 18 Mask** | $1.0$ on TH18 $4\times 4$ tiles, $0.0$ elsewhere | Pinpoints the Town Hall 18 core for the 2nd Victory Star & spell-halo protection. |
| `3` | **Wall Integrity** | $\text{HP}_{\text{wall}} / \text{HP}_{\text{wall,max}} \in [0, 1]$ | Closed compartments vs. breached wall segments. |
| `4` | **No-Deploy Red Zone** | $1.0 = \text{forbidden},\; 0.0 = \text{legal green}$ | Dynamic building halo restricting troop, hero, and siege placement. |
| `5` | **Friendly Ground HP** | $\min(\sum_{u \in G} \text{HP}_u / 5000.0, 1.0)$ | Friendly ground unit mass density at tile $(x,y)$. |
| `6` | **Friendly Air HP** | $\min(\sum_{u \in A} \text{HP}_u / 5000.0, 1.0)$ | Friendly air unit mass density at tile $(x,y)$. |
| `7` | **Active Spell Field** | $1.0$ at active spell centers | Active Rage, Heal, Freeze, Invisibility, Poison, Overgrowth, Totem zones. |
| `8` | **Allied Unit Archetype** | $\max_{u \in \text{Ally}(x,y)} \phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ | Dominant friendly unit identity (Swarm `0.10` .. Ranged Core `0.60` .. Healer `0.81` .. Hero `0.99`). |
| `9` | **Enemy Unit Archetype** | $\max_{u \in \text{Enemy}(x,y)} \phi(u) \in [0, 1]$ | Defending CC, Heroes, TH18 Guardians, and **Ground Skeletons (`0.10`) vs. Air Skeletons (`0.28`)**. |
| `10` | **Archer Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Point ground+air defense ($10.0\text{t}$ range, $145\text{ DPS}$). |
| `11` | **Multi-Archer Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | 3-target merged point defense ($10.0\text{t}$ range, $363\text{ DPS}$). |
| `12` | **Ricochet Cannon** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | 2-target bounce ground defense ($9.0\text{t}$ range, $396\text{ DPS}$). |
| `13` | **Cannon** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Ground point defense ($9.0\text{t}$ range, $235\text{ DPS}$). |
| `14` | **Mortar** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Long-range ground splash ($4\text{–}11\text{t}$ deadzone, $56\text{ DPS}$). |
| `15` | **Air Defense** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | High single-target anti-air rockets ($10.0\text{t}$ range, $650\text{ DPS}$). |
| `16` | **Wizard Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Ground+air area splash defense ($7.0\text{t}$ range, $110\text{ DPS}$). |
| `17` | **Super Wizard Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Merged chain-lightning defense hitting up to 15 targets ($8.0\text{t}$ range, $265\text{ DPS}$). |
| `18` | **Air Sweeper** | $0.25 + 0.75 \cdot (\theta_{\text{deg}} / 360^\circ)$ ($2\times 2$) | Encodes both presence and facing angle of the $105^\circ$ pushback cone. |
| `19` | **Hidden Tesla** | $1.0$ when revealed ($2\times 2$), $0.0$ hidden | Strictly $0.0$ until triggered by proximity or $\ge 51\%$ destruction. |
| `20` | **Bomb Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Ground splash defense + lethal death bomb upon destruction. |
| `21` | **X-Bow** | $1.0 = \text{Air+Ground},\; 0.5 = \text{Ground}$ ($3\times 3$) | Distinguishes $11.5\text{t}$ Air+Ground mode from $14.0\text{t}$ Ground-only mode. |
| `22` | **Inferno Tower** | $1.0 = \text{Single},\; 0.5 = \text{Multi}$ ($3\times 3$) | Distinguishes exponential single-target ramp beam from 6-target multi mode. |
| `23` | **Scattershot** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Heavy $90^\circ$ blast-cone splash behind primary target ($3\text{–}10\text{t}$ range). |
| `24` | **Builder's Hut** | $1.0$ while alive ($2\times 2$) | Weaponized turret + spawns repair builder healing nearby structures. |
| `25` | **Spell Tower** | $0.25\text{ Rage},\; 0.50\text{ Poison},\; 0.75\text{ Invis},\; 1.00\text{ EQ}$ | Encodes exact equipped defensive spell mode ($2\times 2$). |
| `26` | **Monolith** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Anti-tank flat + percentage max-HP priority core threat. |
| `27` | **Multi-Gear Tower** | $1.0 = \text{Long Range},\; 0.5 = \text{Fast Attack}$ ($3\times 3$) | Distinguishes long-range sniping mode from fast-attack mode. |
| `28` | **Firespitter** | $0.25 + 0.75 \cdot (\theta_{\text{deg}} / 360^\circ)$ ($3\times 3$) | Encodes both presence and facing angle of the $150^\circ$ piercing fireball cone. |
| `29` | **Revenge Tower** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Escalating TH18 defense activating stages at 5 / 25 / 50 destroyed buildings. |
| `30` | **Hot Candle (Crafted)** | $1.0$ while alive ($3\times 3$) | Season 4 Crafted Defense (`CraftingStation`) firing $6 \to 4 \to 3$ flames as it melts. |
